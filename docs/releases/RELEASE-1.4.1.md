# Frivo 1.4.1

## Faster dictation with Evora

A dictated sentence is now sent about a second and a half after you stop talking, with Auto on. It used to take up to ten seconds.

- **Evora transcribes dictation faster.** Frivo used to ask Evora to work out who was speaking on every dictation clip, which dictation never needed. Now it asks only for the words. The listening panel still gets speaker recognition.
- **Words appear sooner** while you talk.
- **The final transcription starts as soon as you pause**, instead of after the pause and then in line behind the live preview. It's usually back before Frivo decides you've finished the sentence.
- **Auto-send counts from when you stopped talking**, not from when the text arrived.
- **Pausing and carrying on no longer sends half a sentence.** Before, starting to talk again during the auto-send wait could send the first half on its own.
- **A busy Evora is retried** instead of losing the sentence.
